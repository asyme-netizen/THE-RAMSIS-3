About

THE RAMSIS is built as a modular editorial front end: sharp typography, separated files, and pages that can move easily from Google Pages to any other hosting platform.

Mission

A digital broadsheet.

A premium editorial identity inspired by legacy print newspapers and cultural journals.

Structure

Organized for migration.

Shared CSS and JS, separate pages, components folder, and clean relative linking.

Monetization

Ad-ready layout.

You can insert AdSense blocks between article sections, in the sidebar, or before the footer newsletter.

Project Notes

This version is intentionally static and easy to deploy. The next step can be a React or Next.js build, Arabic + English support, and CMS-backed article management.
